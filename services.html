Internal Tools

2 to 4 weeksFixed price
What it is

The process your team runs on spreadsheets, email and chat, rebuilt as one proper app. Requests, approvals, inspections, registers and trackers, each with a clear owner, deadlines that watch themselves, and a full history of who did what.

What's included
  • A workflow built around your real process, not a generic template stretched to fit
  • Role based access, so each person sees exactly what they should act on

Integrations

2 to 4 weeksFixed price
What it is

The systems you already pay for, finally working together. We connect your ERP, CRM, HR, finance and third party tools, including Oracle Fusion, SAP, Salesforce and Zoho, so information is entered once and appears everywhere it is needed.

What's included
  • A documented connection between your systems, tested against your real data

Your data, wherever it lives today, in a database, spreadsheets or another system, turned into one live view your team actually opens, instead of a report someone compiles by hand every week.
