01 Offer 01

Ops App Sprint

2 to 3 weeksFixed price
What it is

One operational workflow, built as a real application and deployed to your team. A violation tracker, a fleet inspection log, an accommodation and room assignment system, whichever single workflow is currently living in a spreadsheet or on paper.

What's included
  • A database built around your actual workflow, not a generic template stretched to fit
  • Role based access, so a site officer, a regional manager and an admin each see exactly what they should
  • A working web application, usable from a phone at the yard or a desktop in the office
  • One integration point, if data needs to reach or arrive from an existing system
  • Documentation your team can hand to a new hire without calling us

Ideal for

A team with one clearly painful workflow and a single owner who can approve the scope.

Not for

Multi department rollouts needing five or more user roles and several approval chains. That's two sprints, scoped and priced as two.

02 Offer 02

Oracle Fusion Bridge

2 to 4 weeksFixed price
What it is

A lightweight application that reads from and writes to your Oracle Fusion instance through its API, built for the one process Fusion was never configured, or never will be configured, to handle natively.

What's included
  • A documented Oracle Fusion API integration, tested against your actual instance, not a sandbox that behaves differently
  • A purpose built interface for the one workflow, not a generic form bolted onto Fusion's own screens
  • Field level validation, so incomplete or malformed data never reaches your system of record
  • A rollback plan and a written record of exactly what changed and where

Ideal for

Teams who need Fusion to reflect reality faster than their implementation partner can schedule it.

Not for

Full module implementations or configuration changes to Fusion itself. That is your ERP partner's job, not ours, and we will say so.

03 Offer 03

Dashboard Sprint

1 to 2 weeksFixed price
What it is

Your existing data, wherever it currently lives, SQL, spreadsheets, or inside another system, turned into one live reporting view your management team actually opens, instead of waiting on a report someone compiles by hand.

What's included
  • One dashboard, built around the three to five metrics that actually come up in your weekly or monthly review
  • A live connection to your existing data source, with no manual export in the middle
  • Role based views, if a site manager and a regional director need different slices of the same data
  • A short walkthrough so whoever owns the dashboard can read it without a legend

Ideal for

Teams who currently spend hours compiling a report before every meeting it is meant to inform.

Not for

A full business intelligence platform with dozens of interconnected reports. That's a larger engagement, worth scoping on its own.

That is what the first call is for. Tell us what is broken and we will tell you honestly which sprint, if any, solves it.
