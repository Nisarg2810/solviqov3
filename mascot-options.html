01 Mascot options

Pick Solviqo's mascot.

The best B2B mascots, like Mailchimp's Freddie, GitHub's Octocat, Duolingo's Duo and Hootsuite's Owly, share four things: a simple shape you can spot at thumbnail size, big expressive eyes, one signature trait, and a clear link to what the brand does. Here are five characters built on those rules. Choose one and we will bring it to life in a video.

OPTION 01

Zip the swift

The fastest bird in the sky, with swept back wings shaped exactly like our arrow.

QuickCheerfulSharp eyed

Signature move

Swoops in, scoops up the messy spreadsheets and drops a finished app on the desk.

Why it works

Swifts literally sweep across the sky, so it ties straight to our Sweep mark and to speed.

OPTION 02

Kiki the mongoose

Small, fearless and brilliant at hunting down problems, magnifier always in hand.

CuriousFearlessDetail obsessed

Signature move

Pops up on its hind legs, magnifier out, and catches the bug nobody else found.

Why it works

A much loved Indian animal and a natural problem solver. Memorable, and a quiet nod to where we are from.

OPTION 03

Vio the fox

Clever, resourceful and naturally the exact colour of our amber.

CleverResourcefulQuietly confident

Signature move

A knowing wink, then a shortcut through the forest nobody else spotted.

Why it works

Foxes mean smart solutions. Its pointed ears echo our chevrons and it wears the brand colour without trying.

OPTION 04

Bix the beaver

Nature's engineer. Builds things that last, and builds them fast.

HardworkingPracticalProud of the craft

Signature move

Hard hat on, wrench in hand, and a happy tail slap every time something ships.

Why it works

Says we build in one glance, and feels warm and trustworthy to the operations teams we sell to.

OPTION 05

Arro the arrow

The arrow from our logo with a face, and its two little friends always right behind.

EagerUpbeatAlways moving forward

Signature move

Bounces ahead to point the way, and the whole little crew hops after it.

Why it works

The most ownable choice: nobody else can have it, and it turns the logo itself into the character.
