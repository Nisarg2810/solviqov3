02 The gap

Somewhere between your ERP and your ops team, the work falls through.

Oracle Fusion can do almost anything, eventually. Your HSSE manager needs a violation logged today. So the real operation runs on spreadsheets, group chats and someone's memory, while the system of record collects dust.

01

The six month queue

Your Fusion partner prices work around module rollouts. A one screen request sits behind bigger contracts for months, not from neglect, but because that is how the incentives are built.

02

The spreadsheet operation

Violations, inspections and room assignments tracked in files that live on one laptop and break the moment two people edit at once. Nobody trusts the numbers, so everybody checks them twice.

03

The invisible approval

Sign off happens over email and chat. Nobody can say, with certainty and a timestamp, what was approved, by whom, and against which version of the request.
